# AI Dictionary

The project maintains one canonical dictionary for people who use coding agents
and people who build agent systems. Navigation may differ, but definitions do
not.

## Language

**Term**:
A canonical dictionary entry with one definition and one stable name.
_Avoid_: Entry, concept, word

**Category**:
The single subject area that owns a Term. Categories organize the dictionary by
meaning rather than audience.
_Avoid_: Section, track

**Track**:
An audience-specific route through Terms. A Term may belong to the `coding`
Track, the `agent-systems` Track, or both.
_Avoid_: Dictionary, category, edition

**Term status**:
The provenance or maturity of a Term: `established`, `emerging`, `vendor`,
`project`, or `heuristic`.
_Avoid_: Quality, confidence

**Level**:
The assumed reader depth for a Term: `foundational`, `intermediate`, or
`advanced`.
_Avoid_: Importance, priority

**Curriculum**:
The canonical ordered list of all Terms, grouped by Category. Track views filter
this order rather than maintaining separate copies.
_Avoid_: Category, dictionary
